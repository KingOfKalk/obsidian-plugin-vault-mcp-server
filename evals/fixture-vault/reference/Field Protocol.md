# Field Protocol

Standard procedure for every site visit. #procedure

## Calibration

Calibrate every probe against the reference gauge before use.

### Pressure sensors

Zero the sensor at the surface, then lower it to the marked depth and record the offset. ^calib-offset

### Temperature probes

Compare against the reference thermometer in an ice bath.

## Sampling

Take three readings per station, five minutes apart.

## Storage

Keep loggers dry and below 30 degrees Celsius.
